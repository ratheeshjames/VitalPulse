    How BMI works

    BMI is weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared. It screens for weight categories but doesn’t measure body fat, muscle, or where fat is stored.

    FAQ

    Is BMI accurate for athletes?

    Not always. Muscle weighs more than fat, so muscular people can score as overweight.

    Why are Asian cutoffs different?

    At the same BMI, many Asian populations carry more body fat and higher health risk, so lower cutoffs are suggested.

    How are children measured?

    For ages 2–19, BMI is compared with age-based percentiles. This app shows a simplified estimate only.

    Where is my data stored?

    Only in this browser. Nothing is uploaded. Use the backup tools on the Track tab to move it.
